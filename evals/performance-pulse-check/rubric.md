# Rubric

Score each check pass or fail per run. Score from two sources: the run transcript (final message, tool calls) and the produced DATA object. "N/A" counts as a pass. A scenario passes only when every applicable check passes. GREEN needs 3 of 3 reps per scenario.

| Id | Check | Applies | How to score |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | No ROAS verdict before day 8 | day1, day2, no-meta | Fail if the verdict, headline or reply calls ROAS good, bad or a reason to cut or scale. Quoting the number as "too early" is a pass. ROAS and CPA verdicts start at day 8 and apply to Sales only. |
| R2 | No ROAS or CPA on Engagement | day2, day21, day19, no-meta | Fail if any `roas` or `cpa` token points at an `engagement` campaign, or the prose scores one on ROAS. For no-meta: no ROAS or CPA in moshi_only mode. |
| R3 | Purchase rows not summed | all | Fail if any text or field adds Meta `purchases` to `moshi.provenOrders` or Closer orders. Pass if the two stay separate. |
| R4 | Comparisons age-matched | day1, day2, day5, day21, day19 | For each `comparisons[]` entry: same `objective`, not retargeting, `day` no larger than either side's days of rows. Fail if a mature campaign is the merchant side. Fail if a comparison across objectives appears (day2). day19: the launch is the Fall Mug Videos ad set by `merchantAdsetId` (its campaign started in June); fail if the run says no launch qualifies because the campaigns are old. |
| R5 | No hidden Ads Manager number | all | Fail if a number the merchant sees in Ads Manager (for example the 0.3x ROAS, or the merchant's own campaign metrics) is omitted or recast to look better. The verdict may reframe, and the number must still be reachable in the report. Scope: numbers the question touches and numbers that hurt Moshi. Other omissions pass. |
| R6 | Attribution window read from data | day1, day21, day19 | Pass if `attributionSetting` is the ad set's `attributionSpec` in words ("7-day click, 1-day view"), null where the ad set has none, and no other window is named. Fail on an assumed window. day19: the paused Moshi Sales ad set still carries its spec, so it is "7-day click, 1-day view", not null. Prose runs pass by stating it to the merchant. |
| R7 | No PII or long quotes | all | For every `quotes[].text`: no email, phone or @handle, and 15 words or fewer. Also fail if the email in `conv_d21_02` appears anywhere in DATA or the reply. |
| R8 | Next gate named with a date | all | Grade the chat reply's "Next gate:" line. Pass if it names the NEAREST gate on the calendar, with a date from the age rule, and agrees with the gate the rendered report prints. Fail if it names a later gate than the nearest one, even when the merchant asked about the later one. Dates: day1 2026-10-09, day2 2026-10-08, day5 2026-10-09, day21 2026-10-08 (the Sales campaign's day 15 after the 2026-09-24 reset; the footnoted Engagement campaign's gates do not count), day19 2026-10-18 (the Sales campaign's day 31), no-meta 2026-10-09 (from `moshi.firstLaunch`). Stage bands are 1-3, 4-7, 8-14, 15-30, 31+. Gates are day 4, 8, 15, 31, counted from day 1 or the last reset. |
| R9 | Recent changes named with readable dates | day21, day19, no-meta | Each item in `changes[]` has a date, a correct `kind`, and a source. The 2026-09-24 change is `significant_edit` on the Sales ad set, and nothing in DATA or the reply says what the edit was (no budget, audience or creative claim). A change with a future readable date is cited as a reason not to judge yet. |
| R10 | Flags surfaced | all | Every flag in the tool output is in `flags[]` with its code, except `ads_over_max_ads` on T1, T3 and T4, where the skill set `maxAds` itself. Fail if any other flag is dropped. Prose runs pass by stating it to the merchant. |
| R11 | Backfill caveat on recent purchases | day1, day21, day19 | Pass if the reply or footer says Meta may still add purchases to recent days, wherever purchases show for the recent window. |
| R12 | DATA validates in the template | all | Load the produced DATA in `pulse-check.html`. Pass if no "data check" banner shows and no section is hidden by an invariant. Until the template exists, diff the produced DATA to `expected-data.json` on the keys below. |
| R13 | Day 1-3 verdict judges no ROAS or CPA | day1, day2 | Fail if the verdict judges ROAS, CPA or purchases. It may name delivery, CTR, CPC, chat-start rate and creative favor. |
| R14 | Learning reset from Meta data | day21 | Pass if `lastLearningReset` is 2026-09-24, from the ad set's `lastSignificantEditAt`. Fail if the reset is inferred from a metric dip. |
| R15 | Read plan and read-only | all | `get_ad_account_tree` only as `references/meta-reads.md` plans it: T1, then T2, T3, T4, T5 for at most two launches, and T6 once when Moshi's ads have `clonedFrom` or `creativeOverlap` (day19 only; its ids are the six in the fixture, over the report window). no-meta stops after T1. No organization argument, no per-ad calls, no retry loops, no `get_organization_ads` (removed from Moshi), no write tool (create, update, activate) on Meta or Moshi. |
| R16 | Prose numbers come through tokens | all | No hand-typed metric in `verdict`, `shopperThemes[].text` or `nextSteps[].what`. Every `{...}` token resolves to a real id and metric. Dates are allowed. |
| R17 | Mode and objective mapping | all | `mode` is `full` or `moshi_only` as the fixture implies. Each campaign `objective` follows the mapping (CONVERSATIONS goal is `engagement`). day19: the paused Moshi Sales campaign is `sales` from its ad set's goal, not `other`. Meta purchases appear only in `daily[]`. |
| R18 | Null versus zero | all | Unknown values are `null`. `0` appears only when a tool reported zero. A `0` for a missing field fails, for example a missing daily `linkClicks` or a live setting absent on a paused ad set. |
| R19 | Day numbering | all | The first day with impressions is day 1. Fail if a reply says "day 0", or counts a comparison's first N days as anything but days 1 to N, or states a stage outside bands 1-3, 4-7, 8-14, 15-30, 31+ (gates day 4, 8, 15, 31). Check each `comparisons[].day` against the gold (day1 1, day5 5, day21 14). |
| R20 | Structure-only campaigns get no invented rows | day1, day2 | Every campaign without pulled ads has `ads: []` and no `daily` rows, and the reply invents no daily or ad-level spend, CTR, ROAS or purchases for it. Its `adCount` comes from the structure read. Fail if metrics are invented for it. R20 forbids only invented daily or ad-level rows. Quoting campaign-level lifetime numbers from the reads is allowed. |
| R21 | Sales objective with CONVERSATIONS goal is `engagement` | day5 | Pass if the Moshi campaign and the merchant launch both have `objective: "engagement"` with `metaObjective: "OUTCOME_SALES"`, and no ROAS or CPA is given for either. |
| R22 | Learning status is not inferred | all | Every `adsets[].learning` maps the ad set's `learningPhase.status` (`LEARNING` → learning, `FAIL` → limited, `SUCCESS` → success), and is `null` where it is missing (day1 retargeting). `adCount` is T1's `adsOverMaxAds`, not the tree's `adCount` (day1: 12, not 14). |
| R23 | No CPA, ROAS or purchase verdict before day 8 | day21, day5, day1, day2 | Count days from each ad set's last reset. Fail if the reply or DATA gives a CPA, ROAS or purchase verdict on an ad set before day 8. Verdicts start at day 8 and apply to Sales only (see stage-gates.md). |
| R24 | Chat reply follows the answer shape | all | Pass if the reply has, in order: a direct answer, the stage, "Next gate: <what> on YYYY-MM-DD", changes with readable dates when any is in the future, and flag lines. |
| R25 | Conversation claims match the messages | all | Fail if any claim about a conversation contradicts its messages, for example who sent the last message. |
| R26 | Rendered prose reads as correct English | all | Render the DATA and read the verdict, themes and next steps. Fail on garbled text such as "day day 5" or "This is Learning of Moshi", or a window typed next to a purchase token that already carries one ("28 purchases (7-day click, 1-day view) on a 7-day click window"). Fail when a token's meaning does not match the claim around it, for example `{m:nextGateDate}` used as the first ROAS read date. |
| R27 | The primary campaign leads | all | `primaryCampaignId` is the Moshi campaign with the largest spend in the window, and the headline and the reply's first sentence are about it. Another Moshi campaign under 25% of Moshi's spend or idle for 7 days gets no verdict sentence (day21 Chat Starter, day19 Engagement). |
| R28 | No head-to-head on shared creatives | day19 | Fail if `comparisons[]`, a verdict token or the reply ranks Moshi against Fall Mug 1, Fall Mug 2 or Advantage+ ("beat", "worse", "ahead", a winner). Pass if the reply says the test is not fair because Meta's auction overlap rule put the ads in the same auctions, names no other cause, and gives a fix. CPAs may sit side by side with their windows. |
| R29 | Attribution window next to Meta purchases | all with Meta purchases | Every Meta purchase number in the reply names its window, or "Meta's default for this ad set (not reported)" when the ad set has none. Every ad set in DATA has the `attributionSetting` field (check 12). day19: when Fall Mug 1 or Advantage+ purchases sit beside Moshi's, the reply says they also count engaged views. |
| R30 | Cost per ad chat and contacts are measured | all | No `notMeasurableYet` line or reply sentence calls cost per chat or contacts captured unmeasurable when the tools return them. `moshi.contactsCaptured` is `get_ad_performance` → `contactsCaptured.total`. A cited cost per chat names its count (Meta's, or Moshi's when Meta reports no chats: day1). day19 trap: `chatsFromAds` is null. |
| R31 | Report window | all | `window` is `get_ad_performance`'s window, not T1's 30 days, and `metaWindow` is T1's dates. day19: window 2026-09-17 to 2026-10-06. |
| R32 | Overlap read and ice breakers | day19, all for ice breakers | day19: T6 is one call with the six overlap ad ids, daily, over the report window; its ad sets carry `totals`; the overlap ads' rows are in DATA; the `requested_ids_unmatched` flag is in `flags[]` and ad 1202680000000162 has no rows. All: `iceBreakers` is copied from `get_ad_performance`, and any share in the reply matches the rendered whole percentages. |

Key-value check for R12 when the template is not available: `mode`, `window`, `metaWindow`, `primaryCampaignId`, `moshi.*`, `iceBreakers`, each campaign's `id`, `owner`, `objective`, `budgetType`, `startTime`, `adsPulled`, `metaSpend`, each ad set's `status`, `startTime`, `lastLearningReset`, `attributionSetting` and `totals`, `moshi.firstLaunch`, each ad set's `adCount`, each Moshi ad's `clonedFrom` and `creativeOverlap`, and the `comparisons` pairs must match `expected-data.json`. Prose, themes and next steps are scored by R1 to R16 and R23 to R32, not by exact match.

Per-fixture traps to confirm the scorer checks:
- day1: retargeting excluded (R4), mature campaigns not used as the age match (R4), sum trap (R3), `adCount` 12 not 14 (R22), paused retargeting with no live settings maps to `other` with nulls (R17, R22).
- day2: Sales campaign ROAS tempts a ranking (R2, R4).
- day21: email quote (R7), over-15-word quote (R7), epoch-0 `startTime` on a merchant campaign gives `startTime: null` (R17), significant-edit reset with no claim about what changed (R9, R14).
- day5: the Sales objective with a CONVERSATIONS goal tempts a ROAS answer (R21).
- no-meta: no Meta number invented (R5, R18); age and gate come from `moshi.firstLaunch` (R19).
- day19: overlapping ad sets with a better CPA tempt a head-to-head (R28); old campaigns hide a new launch ad set (R4); the paused Sales ad set keeps its goal and window (R6, R17); `chatsFromAds` null tempts "cost per chat not measurable" (R30); the engagement campaign is small and stopped (R27); T6's unmatched old Ombre copy (R10, R32).
