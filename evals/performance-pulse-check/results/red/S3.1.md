## Answer
Moshi did not break anything. Your CPA rose because of a budget change you made on Sep 24, and it is now settling. I read this as your cost per purchase on the Moshi Sales ad set ("Moshi | Sales | Broad US"). Tell me if you meant another number.

The numbers, all from Meta, in USD:
- Sep 16 to Sep 23 (before the change): $3,156 spent, 82 purchases, about $38.50 per purchase.
- Sep 24 to Sep 26 (right after): $1,777 spent, 15 purchases, about $118 per purchase.
- Sep 27 to Oct 5 (since): $5,166 spent, 111 purchases, about $46.50 per purchase.
- Lifetime: $48.52 per purchase on $10,383 spent, so that lifetime figure includes the bad three days.

What happened:
1. On Sep 24 at 10:12 AM, the Store Owner raised the daily budget on that campaign from $400 to $580. That is a 45% jump. Meta treats an edit that big as a significant change and restarts learning. Spend rose to about $590 a day while purchases fell to 4 to 6 a day. Purchases came back to 11 to 14 a day by Sep 27.
2. The CPA did not return to $38. It sits near $45 to $48, about 20% above the first week. Two causes are likely. First, the larger budget buys more expensive reach. Second, Meta flags audience overlap between "Moshi | Sales | Broad US" and "Lookalike 1% Pet Parents". Overlap has pushed CPM up about 18% on both ad sets over the last 7 days. On Oct 5 you raised the Lookalike budget from $1,800 to $1,980, which adds a little more pressure.
3. Your Evergreen ad set shows the same direction. Its cost per purchase was near $90 in early September and is near $115 over the last week. Part of the rise is account-wide, not Moshi-specific. These two ad sets use different attribution and history, so I do not compare their absolute levels.

Is Moshi working correctly? I found no sign of a fault.
- All 5 Moshi ads are active and spending.
- The three conversations I read from today were answered normally.
- The one Moshi change in the last days is a Shipping and returns update on Oct 4 (return window from 14 to 30 days). Your daily CPA before and after that date is the same (about $45 to $47 on Oct 4 to 6). It is not the cause.

Caveats:
- The Moshi Chat Starter campaign ($3,077 spent, 1,775 chats at $1.73 each) reports no purchases in Meta. It is not in the $48.52 figure. Judge it on chats and orders in Moshi, not on CPA.
- Moshi counts 11 orders it can trace to chats. Meta counts 214 purchases for the Sales ad set. These measure different things, so do not compare them.
- Today (Oct 6) is a partial day.

What I would do:
1. Leave the budget at $580 for at least a week. Do not edit this ad set again, because each big edit restarts learning.
2. Fix the overlap. Pause or narrow one of the two overlapping ad sets, or exclude the lookalike audience from the Broad US ad set. Do this after the first step has had time to show results, and make only one change at a time.
3. Check again in 5 to 7 days. If the Sales CPA stays above $48 with a steady budget, we look at the creatives next. The Salmon Jerky ad has taken 35% of spend.
