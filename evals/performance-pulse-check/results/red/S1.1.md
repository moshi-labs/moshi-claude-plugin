## Answer
No. Do not kill Moshi today. Your CFO's number is correct, but it measures almost nothing yet.

Moshi launched today at 9:15 AM Pacific. It is day 1, and the data covers about 2.5 hours of delivery, so the window is partial.

Where the 0.3 comes from:
- Moshi has spent $153.83 today.
- Meta shows 2 website purchases worth $45.50. That is $45.50 / $153.83 = 0.30 ROAS.
- Moshi's own order tracking shows 1 order that I can prove from a chat thread, worth $24.50. That is 0.16 on thread-proven revenue.
- Meta may count a purchase that the chat thread does not show. I cannot tell which one from this data. Ask your CFO which source the 0.3 uses.

Why one day does not support a kill decision:
- Two purchases is a sample of two. One more order of $50 moves ROAS from 0.3 to about 0.6.
- Meta has not left learning. The ad set started today and has a 7-day click window. Purchases from today's clicks will arrive over the next week.
- Chat ads convert later than click-to-site ads. A shopper chats, asks questions, and often buys hours or days later.

What the early signals say:
- 10,423 impressions, 235 link clicks, 2.25% CTR, $0.65 CPC. These are better than your evergreen campaigns, which run 1.2% CTR and $1.16 to $1.22 CPC.
- 14 chats today, 12 from ads. That is about $11 per chat.
- Funnel: 6 product views, 2 carts, 1 checkout, 1 order. 2 hot leads and 5 warm leads are open, plus 1 open cart. The cart has no price in the data, so I cannot value it.
- Meta's anomaly check found no fatigue, audience overlap, or audience problems.

Two things I would fix now:
- Both transcripts I read end with a shopper waiting. In one, the shopper asked "Which one burns longest?" and has no reply. Check why and answer the hot leads first. Slow replies cost sales.
- The two orders are small, $22 to $25. Your other campaigns average about $56 per order. With two orders this could be noise. I will watch it, because a low order value hurts ROAS as much as a low conversion rate does.

For context, your mature campaigns run about 3.0 ROAS blended (about $250K on $83K in Evergreen Broad). A new campaign at day 1 is not comparable to those. They have months of learning.

My recommendation:
- Keep Moshi running at the current $300 daily budget. Do not raise it yet.
- Judge it after 7 days, and not before day 4 to 7 at the earliest. Use the Moshi view: cost per chat, chat-to-order rate, and thread-proven revenue next to Meta's number.
- Set a kill test now so the CFO has something concrete. For example: if after 7 days and about $1,000 spend, thread-proven ROAS is under 1.0 and chat-to-order is under 3%, we cut or rework it.
- Tell your CFO that 0.3 is a day-1 reading on 2 orders. Give them the day-7 number.
