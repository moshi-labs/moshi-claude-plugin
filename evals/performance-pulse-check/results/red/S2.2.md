Your Sales campaign has run since February. It spent $17,892 and returned $60,834 in purchase value, which is about 3.4x ROAS at $18.24 per purchase. That is a mature number.

The Moshi campaign launched yesterday. It has spent $176.84 of its $120 daily budget across two days. It has no purchases and no ROAS yet. That is expected on day 2. Its goal is conversations, not purchases.

What the data shows so far:
- 85 conversations started at $2.08 each. Click-through rate is 2.61%, against 1.30% on Sales. CPC is $0.39, against $0.92.

Two things limit this read. Ad-set learning has just started, so delivery and cost will move. And chat-to-purchase takes days. People often buy after they chat, so 0 orders today does not mean 0 revenue.

The two campaigns are not comparable yet. Moving Sales budget to Moshi would put a proven 3.4x campaign at risk for an unproven one. Cutting Moshi now would end the test before it has a result.

What I suggest:
- Leave Sales at $1,200 per day.
- Leave Moshi at $120 per day and do not edit it, so learning is not reset.
- Check back around day 7. Look at orders from chats, revenue, and cost per order, then compare that with Sales' $18.24 per purchase.
- Shift budget only if Moshi cost per order beats or matches Sales at that point.
