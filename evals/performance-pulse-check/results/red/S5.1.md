## Answer
Short version: it is too early for a verdict, and Meta cannot give you ROAS or CPA for this campaign. Moshi can give a first read.

One thing first. The campaign has a Sales objective, but its ad set optimizes for Conversations. Meta counts conversations started as the result, not purchases. So Meta's purchase ROAS and purchase CPA fields are empty. That is expected for this setup. It is not a tracking fault.

What Moshi shows for Oct 2 to Oct 6 (day 5), spend $1,166.14:
- Orders: 2, with $148 revenue.
- ROAS: about 0.13x ($148 / $1,166.14).
- CPA: about $583 per order ($1,166.14 / 2).
- Cost per conversation: $2.76 (423 conversations in Meta, 420 chats from ads in Moshi).
- Funnel: 115 product views, 8 carts, 3 checkouts, 2 orders. 8 carts are open, and their value is not known yet.

How much weight to give these numbers: little. Two orders is too small a sample, and a single order moves CPA by hundreds of dollars. One more order is marked as delayed and is not in the 2. The campaign is on day 5, and chat-to-purchase often takes days, so some orders from these chats have not landed. I would not call ROAS 0.13x a failure on day 5. I would not call it a success either.

Comparison with Summer Pack Sale (launched Aug 7):
- I cannot compare ROAS or CPA. Meta shows no purchase results for the summer campaign, and I have no order or revenue data for it in this session. It also optimized for Conversations, so it has no purchase CPA to compare against.
- What I can compare is the same first 5 days (Aug 7 to Aug 11 for summer, across its 8 ads): $4,481 spend, 702 conversations, about $6.38 per conversation.
- Moshi's first 5 days: $2.76 per conversation, so about 57% cheaper per conversation. Overall campaign numbers also lean Moshi: CTR 2.42% vs 1.25%, CPC $0.51 vs $1.00, CPM $12.35 vs $12.50. The CPM is nearly the same. The savings come from more clicks and chats, not cheaper reach.
- Summer ran at $900/day and Moshi at $250/day, so scale differs a lot. Summer also has 2.6M impressions over two months, against 94K for Moshi.

Cheap conversations only matter if they turn into orders, and that is the open question. Right now 2 orders from 420 ad chats is 0.5%.

What I would do: leave the campaign alone for now, since changes reset learning. Check again around day 10 to 14, when late orders and the 8 open carts have settled. If you want a real summer ROAS comparison, share the summer order revenue from Shopify and I will line it up.
